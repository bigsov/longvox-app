New in 0.2.24:

- Spoken punctuation and commands in English and Russian: "comma", «запятая», "full stop", «точка», "new line", "scratch that", «зачеркни это». Settings › Speech › Every command lists them all.
- Spoken numbers become figures, in English and Russian: "twenty five percent" → 25%, «пятого декабря» → 5 декабря, «трёхсот сорока тысяч рублей» → 340 000 рублей.
- Choose any of the 17 speech models Longvox for Windows offers (Settings › Speech › Speech model).
- Longvox can learn people's voices: in a meeting's transcript, right-click a turn and choose Remember this voice…, and later meetings name that person. Download "Voice recognition" in Settings › Speech › Voices first. Voices are kept in the same file format as Longvox for Windows.
- Ask all meetings at once: the button in the Meetings window's toolbar.
- Speakers who introduce themselves ("I'm Anna", «меня зовут Анна») are named from the calendar invite.
- History and Meetings open from Settings › General and from the Longvox icon in the Dock (right-click), and ⌘, opens Settings, for MacBooks whose notch hides the menu-bar icon.
- When the microphone gives only silence, Longvox now says so and points to System Settings › Sound › Input.

Longvox is larger than before (about 52 MB): voice recognition needs Microsoft's ONNX Runtime.
