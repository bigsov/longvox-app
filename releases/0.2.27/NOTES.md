New in 0.2.27:

- Private calls: pair with Longvox for Windows on your private network and join audio, video and group video calls from this Mac, with a camera and microphone check before you join.
- Preview, for the next Longvox for Windows (0.2.2 does not have these yet): call one of your own devices directly, share a single window during a call, and allow the other person to control that window. You approve each session on this Mac and can stop it at any time. Control needs Screen Recording, Accessibility and Input Monitoring.
- Your devices, directly: pair your Mac with your other Longvox devices using an 8-digit code, and meetings, recordings, voices and corrections sync end-to-end encrypted, with no other network software needed.
- Russian interface: the whole app follows your macOS language, English or Russian.
- Meetings: correct a line of a transcript, rename a speaker's label, and see action items from all meetings in one list.
- Privacy and permission texts now say exactly what each permission is for and what is sent where.
