New in 0.2.26:

- Edit and delete in History. Edit keeps the earlier text; Delete asks first, and the recording goes to the Bin.
- Longvox learns from your edits: correct a misheard word in History and it is fixed automatically from then on, as Longvox for Windows does. Settings › Words › Learn from my edits turns it off.
- Share voices and corrections with Longvox for Windows through your notes folder (OneDrive, iCloud Drive, an Obsidian vault): Settings › General, under Notes folder, with Sync now.
- Settings › Devices: your meetings, recordings, voices and corrections copied directly between your own devices, with no server in between. For now each device needs Tailscale; syncing over the same Wi-Fi without it is coming.
- When a FaceTime call silences the microphone, Longvox names FaceTime.
