First public version of Longvox for Mac.

- Press F9 in any app and speak English, Russian, or both; the text appears at the cursor.
- Two presets: Accurate (Qwen3-ASR 1.7B) and Instant (Parakeet v3), running on your Mac's GPU.
- Optional Russian → English translation while you speak, on-device (Apple Translation).
- Every dictation is kept with its audio in History; "Transcribe again" re-runs any of them.
- Check for Updates in Settings › About.
