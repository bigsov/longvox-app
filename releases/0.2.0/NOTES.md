Meeting recording.

- Record a meeting from the Longvox menu: your microphone and the other side of the call (whatever your Mac plays: Zoom, Teams, FaceTime) are transcribed live as You and Others.
- When you stop, Apple Intelligence on your Mac writes a summary, key points and action items, and names the meeting (an extractive summary for Russian).
- A Meetings window: search everything that was said, play any moment, keep notes, export as text, Markdown or subtitles.
- Needs the System Audio Recording permission the first time (macOS asks).
- Fixed: quitting Longvox could end in a crash report.
